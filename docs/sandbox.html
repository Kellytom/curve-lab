Sandbox

The working pages: draw, measure, compare. Every one is generated from code in this repository.

The curve and the pipeline

  • Spirals that make a dress curve new Log spirals, hyperbolic spirals and a clothoid at the same scale, to match a hip or vary-form curve by eye.
  • Euler spiral: turns and merging new How many times a clothoid winds in a 100 grid, and when the windings touch. Sections 5–8 cover the special case: a spiral curvature-matched to its inner circle (G2), unwinding outward from r = 5 to the page edge at r = 49; the join is the tightest point, and R/√3 is the max-turn radius.
  • Catenary sandbox: triples and drapery new Two projects: a catenary chained from pythagorean triples (two branches, split by percentage), and Weil’s catenary drapery in 2.5D.
  • Clothoid intermediate curves stage II new A spine is a chain of chords; a plate is what you draw from it. Offset parallel curves and why the cubics cannot be offset, then branch-at-every-node growth on one side, alternating and both.
  • Where to branch new Candidate pins on the practical curve, probe branches, and a clearance verdict for each.
  • The practical curve The same study at 34 cubics instead of 1,164, with pins for branch points.
  • Study 01 from chains The exact chain study, every span end measured against P01.
  • Spine on the sheet A chain on 200 × 250 mm, and a declared contact at a forked node.
  • Piece model and terminals Traces, rails, mirror, and the seven kinds of end.
  • Fleur study 01 — the port The old grammar rebuilt as clothoid segments, with node parity.
  • Primal studies Earth, water, air, fire: one grammar each.
  • Principle cards Four primals and four archetypes: gesture, three numbers, not-this.

How to run it

The local app is 127.0.0.1:8000 from .venv/bin/python web/server.py. These pages are static: .venv/bin/python -m http.server 8003 --directory docs, then http://127.0.0.1:8003/sandbox.html. Each page is written by its script in scripts/; edit the script, not the HTML.
