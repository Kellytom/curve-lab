The floor and the old pick, judged on the new set

The rungs below are the ones that looked fine on the old grey plates and do not survive the alpha conversion: the worst alpha error of the 3-colour print is 177–194 steps on blue — that is the banding you saw. The floor rung is gentler on alpha than 3 colours but keeps only 4–5 levels.

Eight more curves at the floor — the wash-out check, rebuilt

Original on the left of each pair, floor on the right. The moved share is the wash-out signal: c-0088 and c-0142 are the worst of this batch. One oddity stands: c-0088's floor output is bigger than its original — dither noise costs more than the palette saves on that plate.

Where this leaves the choice

★ My ready pick: plain 90-99. On the alpha set it is the only rung whose worst alpha error stays at or under ~54 steps with the over-8 share at 0.02–0.05% of pixels, and the whole-set estimate is ~18 MB against the 60.6 MB source. 70-95 is the size option (~12 MB) at four times the alpha error. The floor and the colour-count rungs (3, 2, and the 16-colour cap) all band the transparency and are recorded here so the decision is made with them visible, not remembered.

The commands, so the numbers can be re-derived

Snapshot: copy app/public/plates-web/*.png aside (22:23). Each rung: pngquant [args] --speed 1 --force --output out.png in.png with --quality 70-95, --quality 90-99, --colors 16 --quality 90-99, --quality 0-10, --colors 3, --colors 2. Fidelity: Pillow, RGBA, per-channel difference, grey and alpha separately.
