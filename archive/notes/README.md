# Archive: raw notes

These files are working notes, not documentation.

They were pasted into `docs/` during earlier sessions: conversation excerpts,
prompt dumps, and dated scratch lists. They were never in the book's navigation,
and they are kept here only because they record decisions that are otherwise
hard to reconstruct.

- They are not built by mkdocs.
- They are not a statement of current truth. `docs/` and `state/` are.
- Nothing here should be linked from a reader-facing page.

If a paragraph in one of these notes is still true, it belongs in a proper
document under `docs/`, with a heading, a date and a check.
