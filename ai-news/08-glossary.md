# Glossary — the project's words

- **Curve** — the line itself. Either speed rows (`u`, `v`) or a stored path.
- **Speed rows** — two polynomials `u(t)`, `v(t)`; the position is the integral of
  `(u²−v², 2uv)`. A curve in this form is exact.
- **PH curve** — a curve whose speed is a Pythagorean pair; its offset is rational.
- **Stored path** — a sampled outline; exact at its stations, approximate between them.
- **Grid** — the one denominator that makes every station whole, written `1/G` (e.g. `1/105`).
- **Bend** — arc length over chord length. A plain number, no percent.
- **Crossing pair** — two parameters `t₁ < t₂` at the same place. Three passes = three pairs.
  Never call this "crossings" and mean places.
- **Inflection** — where the curve stops bending one way and starts bending the other.
- **Spine** — the curve as the plate draws it.
- **Side curve / rail** — the line beside the spine at a distance set by the law.
- **Inside / outside** — the inside of a turn is the side toward the centre of curvature; only it
  can fold. The outside keeps the width.
- **Character rail** — a side curve whose distance varies along the curve (bow, s, taper).
- **Fold** — the band crossing itself because the offset exceeded the local radius.
- **Buckle** — the same word the operator uses for a fold; the tuner reports it.
- **Cut point** — where the outside rail crosses itself; a future place to cut the band.
- **Plate** — the printed SVG: white ground, no text, two greys, the spine and its sides.
- **Class V** — the gothic arch: half a Tschirnhausen trimmed, mirrored, joined at the tip.
- **Glue** — a connector between two curves whose end slopes differ.
- **Wonk** — the raw half a double was cut from; the doubles ("dwonkies") are the kept curves.
- **Desk** — the tuning page where the four plate values are set and saved.
- **Register** — `state/REGRESSIONS-int.md`: every fault and the test that keeps it fixed.
- **Workhorse** — the queue that runs slow jobs outside the chat.
