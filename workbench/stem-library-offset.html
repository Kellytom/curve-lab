Stem offsets

One stalk, two rails. The rails stand at ±d, so they are 2d apart; the card gutter is the breathing room. The catalogue shows the exact normal rail where it is exact, Levien's fitted cubic-Bézier parallel (gold dash), and the LAC chain rail.

The rule. Every card is one stalk with two offsets at ±d. A rail folds when d reaches the local centre of curvature; the LAC cards choose d below the minimum radius and report the margin. The Levien fit is an option, never an exact claim: its error is the maximum distance between the fitted cubic chain and the dense exact normal offset. The exact PH offset is the PH rail; the LAC rail is the clothoid-chain fit; the formulaic/conic rails are the sampled normal offset unless the family closes under offsetting.
