Square and frame lab

Squares and rectangles only. One rule: the leg bisects the 90° corner, so it is 45°. An arch joins continuously; an S joins at a right angle. The frame and the leaf are built separately and only share the page.

…

The leaf is curve a below and curve b above, flipped, with the feet forced onto the same two points. The tip angles are printed: 90° / 90° means the two curves meet cleanly at both feet; a mismatch (e.g. 90° / 0°) means one foot is smooth and the other is square, which is the attach problem to watch.

The stroke floor is 1 point at every size: the page measures in points, and strokeFor never returns less. The box is curves across × the chord by curves down × the chord, so 2 × 3 gives a tall frame. The pattern cycles a/b along each side.
