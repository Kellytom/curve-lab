Square and frame lab

One curve, placed on the edges by one rule: the leg bisects the corner. A square corner is 90°, so the leg is 45°; an arch then joins continuously, an S joins at a right angle. The frame and the cross are built separately and only share the page.

…

The stroke floor is 1 point at every size: the page measures in points, and strokeFor never returns less. At 4 in a 0.35% line is 1.008 pt; at 1 in the floor holds it at 1 pt. The frame side is curves per side × the chord, so more copies on the same page means a finer, lace-like scale.
