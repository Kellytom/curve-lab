The three aspects

Linearity: both end tangents are straight, and the control polygon is two triangles against the chord. Curve: the S bend changes the tangent continuously between them. Infinity: absent — a cubic is a bounded arc. A design that uses one of these curves must supply the third aspect elsewhere, with a spiral tail or a ray to a point at infinity. The catalogue is a parts list, not a finished design.

Reading a row. The chord is 2c and both handle arms are c, so the control polygon is two congruent triples against the chord. P1 and P2 are the canonical controls; mirror P1/P2 are the sign variant; rotated P1 is where the triple's own rotation sends P1 at scale c — an integer by construction, which is the exactness the catalogue is for.
