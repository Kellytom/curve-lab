The eleventh power

The septic sandbox stops at position degree 7, where the speed w is cubic. This page is the same machine one step up: r' = w^2 with w of degree 5, so twelve speed numbers and a position of degree 2 × 5 + 1 = 11. The offsets stay rational, the pitch is still the lcm of the integrated coefficients' denominators, and the closure algebra is unchanged — more terms, same rules.

A | the PH curve of degree 11

Six numbers for x and six for y, from t^0 to t^5. The last pair is zero by default, which makes the curve a septic with two unused knobs: lift it and the eleventh power appears. close chord drives the endpoint home; close to … holds a chosen openness instead, because a fully closed curve has no chord and bend is arc over chord.

B | the end: terminal and serif

An offset that flares is just r ± d(t) n(t) with d a function, and for a PH curve the normal is rational, so a polynomial width law keeps the rail rational — the flare is exact, not fitted. The terminal follows the house rule: radius 2 d(1), two arcs tangent to the rails, meeting at the tip. The serif foot is a PH span of the same degree: in the frame of the rail end it leaves along the rail's tangent and arrives along the base, landing on a foot of half width f. Four conditions on a degree-5 speed, solved in the page; the residual is printed beside the width. A circular arc is kept as the fallback when the solve misses by more than 0.05.

One row per position power, x above y, on a shared zero line. The first six rows are what a sextic can hold; the rest exist only because the position reaches t^11. The numbers are in the panel above and in the JSON box below.

What the eleventh power costs

Every extra degree adds one speed pair and two position powers. The closure condition stays two equations, so the closed family grows by two dimensions per degree — more room for loops, which is what the septic already showed over the quintic. The price is the pitch: the integrated coefficients carry 1/(k+1) and the squares of the decimal denominators, so the integer scale climbs faster than the shape count. The low-pitch form of each saved curve is the same shape on a coarser grid; it is a change of units, not a new curve.

The flare is the reason for this page: a serif is a width story, not a curvature story. The rail distance is a declared number, and the terminal radius is twice it, so flare, terminal and foot are one number apart rather than three drawings agreeing by eye.
