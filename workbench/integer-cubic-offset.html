Integer cubic offset

The controls are integers; the offset is not. The rail below is a fitted cubic chain, and the page says so.

The source. The 3-4-5 cubic has P0 = (0, 0), P1 = (3, -4), P2 = (7, 4), P3 = (10, 0): integer controls, chord 10, both arms 5, tangent -53.13°. It is exact as a cubic and it is not a Pythagorean-hodograph curve.

The rail. Levien's method samples the true normal offset and fits a cubic chain at distance 0.5. The maximum error is 1.94e-03 over 8 fitted spans. The dashed line is the dense exact offset, drawn for comparison only — the fitted chain is the deliverable, and it is labelled fitted.

Exact integer controls do not imply an exact offset. The corner of that distinction is the whole reason the offset catalogue has a fitted tier.

What this rules out. Calling this an exact offset because the source controls are integers. The exact routes are lines, arcs, PH curves, the Pythagorean spiral and the Pythagorean integer cubic family's own exact objects; a general integer cubic is not one of them.
