Plant forms

One stalk, two rails, an ending, marks at the integer lattice, and branches that get smaller upward.

The sequence. The stalk is offset at ±d; the rails are 2d apart. Every stalk ends in a flower, a seagrass/leaf ending, or — if there is no flower or head — a cone terminal: two arcs of radius 2 × offset, one from each rail, meeting at the centreline. The growth mark is a small pyramid on each rail at an integer-lattice crossing. Branches leave selected marks; the lower branch is longer than the upper, and each branch gets its own ending. No bare tip.

The grid is the integer lattice in model space. The stalk crosses the horizontal integer lines at the marked points; the pyramid’s base sits on the rail and its apex points outward.
