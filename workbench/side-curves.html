Side curves: per-construction partners

The side curves are the same construction as the spine with one parameter changed: a smaller construction on the right (parameter − d_R) and a larger one on the left (parameter + d_L). No width offset, no normal estimate, no curvature cap. d3 draws the curves, axes and cap lines; the overlapping dots are only the stroke. The construction's centre is the origin.
