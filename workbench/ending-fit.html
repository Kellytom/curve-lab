Ending fit pass

Every C-family section attached to one stalk tip, at a fixed 6 × offset reach, with the numbers that decide whether it can be an ending.

The fit. The stalk is vertical, width 1, so the rails stand at ±0.5. Each curve's first point is placed on the tip and its first tangent turned onto the stalk; it is then scaled so its farthest point is 3 from the tip (6 × offset) and mirrored into a two-sided ending. The 2 × offset rule is untouched: the ending grows beyond the tip, it does not narrow the stalk.

What is measured. The scale factor, the reach, the minimum clearance from the ending to either rail, the number of cusps (turns over 150°), and the number of segment crossings. A card is flagged only when a measured crossing contradicts its grade; the ending grades in TODO.md stand, because a fit is evidence, not a promotion.
