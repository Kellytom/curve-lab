Part 2: fitted curves

Part 1 carries curves whose speeds are the curve. This page carries fitted curves: each showcase path sampled, its speed taken as the square root of the derivative, a degree-5 polynomial fitted, and the residual recorded. The sharpest fits come first. If one is worth keeping, it moves to part 1 by name.

The old text follows for reference. The septic sandbox stops at position degree 7, where the speed w is cubic. This page is the same machine one step up: r' = w^2 with w of degree 5, so twelve speed numbers and a position of degree 2 × 5 + 1 = 11. The offsets stay rational, the pitch is still the lcm of the integrated coefficients' denominators, and the closure algebra is unchanged — more terms, same rules.

A | the PH curve of degree 11

Six numbers for x and six for y, from t^0 to t^5. The last pair is zero by default, which makes the curve a septic with two unused knobs: lift it and the eleventh power appears. close chord drives the endpoint home; close to … holds a chosen openness instead, because a fully closed curve has no chord and bend is arc over chord.

The offsets are parallel here: r ± d n with d constant. Character offsets keeps the same construction but turns the speed a little at each station — g = w (1 + i ε b(t)) — and blends back to the parallel at both ends, so the rail leaves and arrives at exactly ± d and bows in between. The two character dials set how far each side bows (they can differ, which is what a serif needs), the profile chooses the bow's shape (one swell, an S that crosses the parallel, or a taper toward the end), end shear leans the rails along the tangent by the end, and tail hook adds h e^{iφ} t^5 to the speed itself, bending the last span without leaving the PH family. The flare law and the serif foot are parked in ph-nth-serif-notes.html.

What the eleventh power costs

Every extra degree adds one speed pair and two position powers. The closure condition stays two equations, so the closed family grows by two dimensions per degree — more room for loops, which is what the septic already showed over the quintic. The price is the pitch: the integrated coefficients carry 1/(k+1) and the squares of the decimal denominators, so the integer scale climbs faster than the shape count. The low-pitch form of each saved curve is the same shape on a coarser grid; it is a change of units, not a new curve.

The flare is the reason for this page: a serif is a width story, not a curvature story. The rail distance is a declared number, and the terminal radius is twice it, so flare, terminal and foot are one number apart rather than three drawings agreeing by eye.
