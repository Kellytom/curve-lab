Euler sections from C curves

The p-triple route: a rational Euler polygon, exact rational miter rails, and a cubic seam for the canvas.

The order. The C curve is sampled at equal arc length and rotated so its first chord is east. A primitive triangle angle is chosen with for_angle; each chord's heading change is quantized to whole triangle turns. The resulting Spiral is the exact rational Euler polygon. Its rails are the exact rational miter offsets from ribbon.rails; the cubic seam from spans.spans is the drawing approximation. Grey = target C curve, ink = polygon, blue/red = rails, gold = cubic seam.

The polygon and its rails are exact. The target curve and the cubic drawing are approximations; the card carries both margins. Cusps and loops are split before conversion; these cards show one smooth section each. The full family still needs the cusp/loop splits.
