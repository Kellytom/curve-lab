Two-parabola S probe

Two quadratics can make a clean S with a G1 join. They cannot make a G2 S across a curvature reversal: a nondegenerate parabola has one curvature sign, and the two halves meet with opposite signed curvature.

join tangent(10,−10)

G2no — curvature sign reverses

What this means

Degree elevation is exact, but it does not add a curvature reversal. A single parabola has one curvature sign. Joining two parabolas across the middle can give a visual S with a tangent match; the curvature jumps from one sign to the other. That is a G1 joint, not a G2 joint.

The integer cubic family is the right home for an exact S with a genuine inflection. Keep quadratic elevation for a one-bend arc, or for a piecewise-parabolic S that accepts a measured G1 curvature jump.
