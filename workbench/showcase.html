Rays and curves

Every family this project has built, in the order it was solved: the integer Euler polygon first, then the LACs, the integer chains, the widths, the composition — and the rays last, where they belong on a monstrance. Panels are lettered a, b, c inside each numbered figure, and every figure is a link to its notes.

Key. exact construction · exact rail · integer or fitted mark · approximate or exceptional. Kind (on each note): exact, fitted, negative, asymptotic, drawn form.

Not on this page

deliberate absences

No constant-width stem, because no exact one exists here; no parallel parabola, because the parallels are involutes of the evolute and belong to another family; no squircle offsets, because the curve is not rational; no single clothoid claiming G2, because one cannot do it. Each absence is a result, not an omission.

Figures are drawn to their own extents; axes and units are inside each panel. Red marks the approximate or the exceptional, blue the exact rail, gold the integer construction.
