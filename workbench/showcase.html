Rays and curves

Every family this project has built, in the order it was solved: the integer Euler polygon first, then the LACs, the integer chains, the widths, the composition — and the rays last, where they belong on a monstrance. Panels are lettered a, b, c inside each numbered figure.

1 · Integer Euler

exact turns, exact closure, rational rails; only the drawn cubic is fitted

2 · LAC spines

31 recovered spines; the turn schedule is the curve

3 · Integer Bézier · Euclid family chains

integer control points at scale 1 · C1 exact · exponent −4/3 in the limit

4 · Widths

one exact taper, and two constructions that are provably not what they look like

5 · Composition

parts on different grids, a declared step, and an ending

6 · Rays

the p-triple angles themselves, mirrored about the axis
