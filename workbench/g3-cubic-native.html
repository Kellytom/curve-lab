Curves that collapse to cubics

A quintic span is an exact elevation of a cubic when both the fourth and fifth finite differences of its control polygon vanish: d4 = P0 − 4P1 + 6P2 − 4P3 + P4 (and the shifted one), and d5 = P0 − 5P1 + 10P2 − 10P3 + 5P4 − P5. The residual is max(|d4|, |d5|) / chord; a four-point source span is cubic by construction. The showcase and the notes are the primary pages.
