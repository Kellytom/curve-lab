curve-lab · the missing families

Six families that were absent, and what each is

Every family here was named as missing in the audit. Each is drawn from its own definition, with the exactness status stated: the elastica and the spirals are fitted; the spline interpolators are constructions; the rational PH circle and the degree-7 PH curve carry exact controls after one scale.

1 · Euler elastica · κ′ = ±½√(m⁴−κ⁴)

The energy ∫κ²ds is minimised by this family. Curvature is bounded, the curve inflects, and it loops as m rises. Fitted: elliptic, no exact lattice route claimed.

2 · Polynomial spirals · κ(s) = s^p

The clothoid is the linear case. Higher p gives a faster turn for the same arc length; all are transcendental and fitted.

3 · Sinusoidal spirals · r^n = a·cos(nθ)

One dial contains the circle, the cardioid, a parabola and the lemniscate. Fitted; the members were already in the catalogue, the family was not.

4 · General trochoids · k = R/r

Four k values were in the catalogue (deltoid, nephroid, epicycloids); this is the dial. Fitted; the roulettes are transcendental unless the rational parametrisation is used.

5 · Spline interpolators

Catmull–Rom interpolates its control points at C1; the uniform B-spline is C2 but does not interpolate them; the rational quadratic with w = 1/√2 is an exact circular arc; the quintic Hermite span matches position, tangent and acceleration at both ends, so a join is G2 by construction. These are the interpolator families, not new curves.

6 · Rational PH and degree-7 PH

The circle is a rational PH curve: its speed is the rational function 2/(1+t²), so its offsets are exact. The degree-7 curve comes straight from the degree lemma with u, v cubic and h constant; its six-plus-one controls are integers after the stated scale.

Status

All six are fitted or constructions except the PH pair, where the controls are integer at the scale. None is added to NAMED: the elastica, the spirals and the trochoids are transcendental; the interpolators are methods; the rational circle is a conic already present. They are declared here so the family list is closed as far as it can be.
