Heart forms

Five heart forms, each drawn to its true extent. The Beutel sextic is the one the engine samples; the other four are here to read against it before the French-curve and clothoid work begins.

What is on the page. The cardioid is the one rational member here. The |x| heart and the two closed parametric forms are sampled, because they are not polynomial rational curves in the engine's sense. No form is fitted, measured against a reference, or spliced at this stage; the page is a catalogue, not a claim.

Beutel sextic

(x² + y² − 1)³ = x² y³

sextic · genus 4 · three real cusps · sampled

Cardioid

r = 1 + sin θ

rational · one cusp, at the bottom · exact

|x| heart

x² + (y − |x|²ᐟ³)² = 1

two sampled branches · cusps at the cleft and the point

Fourier Valentine

x = 16 sin³ t, y = 13 cos t − 5 cos 2t − 2 cos 3t − cos 4t

Fourier series · closed · sampled

Polar heart

r = 2 − 2 sin θ + sin θ √|cos θ| / (sin θ + 1.4)

polar · after MathWorld's heart curve · sampled
