Heart forms

Fifteen formulaic hearts, each drawn to its true extent. The Beutel sextic is the one the engine samples; the rest are here to read against it before the French-curve and clothoid work begins.

What is on the page. The cardioid is the one rational member here. The rest are sampled: the implicit forms because they are not rational in the engine's sense, the parametric and polar forms because they are transcendental or piecewise. No form is fitted, measured against a reference, or spliced at this stage; the page is a catalogue, not a claim.

Sources. The implicit, parametric and polar forms are from MathWorld's Heart Curve; the explicit and composite forms are from Torsten Sillke's collection. The original names are on each card.

Beutel sextic

(x² + y² − 1)³ = x² y³

implicit · sextic · genus 4 · three real cusps · sampled
E. Beutel 1909 · MathWorld (1)

Cardioid

r = 1 + sin θ

polar · rational · one cusp, at the bottom · exact
MathWorld (11)

|x| heart

x² + (y − |x|²ᐟ³)² = 1

implicit · two sampled branches · cusps at the cleft and the point
J. Schroeder · MathWorld (2)

Fourier Valentine

x = 16 sin³ t, y = 13 cos t − 5 cos 2t − 2 cos 3t − cos 4t

parametric · Fourier series · closed · sampled
MathWorld (5–6)

Polar heart

r = 2 − 2 sin θ + sin θ √|cos θ| / (sin θ + 1.4)

polar · after MathWorld's anonymous polar heart · sampled
MathWorld (12)

Kuriscak heart

x² + [y − 2(x²+|x|−6)/(3(x²+|x|+2))]² = 36

implicit · two sampled branches · the Kuriscak heart
P. Kuriscak · MathWorld (4)

Wu/Nick heart

x² + y² − |x| y = 1

implicit · the right half of an ellipse plus its reflection
F. Wu 2025 · Nick 2014 · MathWorld (13–14)

Mangaldan heart

x = −√2 sin³ t, y = 2 cos t − cos² t − cos³ t

parametric · a nephroid modified into a heart · closed
J. Mangaldan 2023 · MathWorld (7–8)

Dascanio heart

x = sin t cos t ln|t|, y = |t|⁰·³ √cos t

parametric · t ∈ [−1, 1] · the two halves meet at the origin
H. Dascanio 2003 · MathWorld (9–10)

Half-ellipses

y = |x| ± √(1 − x²)

explicit · two half-ellipses · the oldest joke heart
N. Herrmann · Sillke collection

Square-root heart

y = √|x| ± √(1 − x²)

explicit · two sampled branches
F. Krause / K.-H. Kriegl · Sillke collection

Eisemann heart

x² + (y − (3/4)|x|²ᐟ³)² = 1

implicit · the |x| heart with a smaller lift
K. Eisemann · Sillke collection

sio heart

x² + 2(y − (3/5)|x|²ᐟ³)² = 1

implicit · a wider |x| heart
sio · Sillke collection

Holzapfel heart

y = √(1−(|x|−1)²), y = arccos(1−|x|) − π

explicit · two semicircles on top, an arccos curve below
M. Holzapfel · Sillke collection

Hechinger heart

y = √(1−(|x|−1)²), y = −3√(1−√(|x|/2))

explicit · two semicircles on top, a root curve below
T. Hechinger · Sillke collection
