Storey 02 · Pillar

A grounded column from the existing parts: a plinth, a torus, an entasised shaft and a capital, on one declared axis.

The parts. The baseline is the ground; the plinth is a 48-wide block; the torus is a pair of mirrored cubics; the shaft is two mirrored cubic profiles with integer controls, swelling to 16.39 at y = 27 before tapering to 9; 4 flutes mark the shaft; the capital is a band, an echinus pair and a 38-wide abacus.

The three aspects. Linearity: the baseline, the plinth and abacus edges, and the flutes. Curve: the entasis profiles and the echinus. Infinity: the axis, drawn past the abacus as a ray — the vertical has no top. Grounded: the baseline at y = 0 is the datum; the vertical is the axis at x = 0. The column stands on the one and rises through the other.

Why it is a storey

The pillar is not a new curve family. It is the part vocabulary at architecture scale: ground, block, torus, profile, flute, echinus, abacus, axis. The profiles are cubics with integer controls and are exact mirrors of one another; the width comes from the profile, so nothing here claims a parallel offset. The piece is grounded and names its three aspects.
