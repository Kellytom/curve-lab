Rosettes from a 45 or 90 degree arc

One arc of 45 or 90 degrees, repeated N times at 360/N about its own centroid. The count under each plate is where neighbouring petals cross. Three or more copies cross once per join, so a rosette of N petals has N meetings; two copies cross twice, which is the lens.

The circle (p = 0) and the log spiral (p = 1) are the two ends of the log-aesthetic family that behave at this turn. Generated by scripts/lac_rosettes.py from scripts/lac_generate.py.
