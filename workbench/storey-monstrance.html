Storey 01 · Monstrance

A vessel from the existing parts: a ground, a stem, a head, a host and a p-triple ray fan.

The parts. The baseline is the ground; the foot is a trapezoid; the stem is a cubic with two rails at ±2.2; the head is a circle of radius 24 with a host of 12; the 24 rays are the p-triple angles 2·atan(1/m), mirrored. A small cross tops it.

The three aspects. Linearity: the baseline and the rays. Curve: the stem and the head. Infinity: the rays continue to a point at infinity. Grounded: the horizontal baseline is the datum; the stem is the vertical. The vessel stands on the one and rises through the other.

Why it is a storey

The monstrance is not a new curve family. It is the part vocabulary used at architecture scale: ground, foot, stem, rails, head, host, rays, cross. The parts are declared, the rays are the same exact p-triple angles as the showcase fan, and the piece is grounded and names its three aspects.
