curve-lab · tightest-span diagnostics

Tightest spans · the closest approaches, in gold

A join-only test can pass two spans that each graze a near-cusp inside: the ends match, but the span nearly stops and dκ/ds spikes by orders of magnitude. The fit now rejects any span below 10% of its chord speed. These 16 working curves keep a span below 25%: none is rejected, but they are the closest approaches that remain. The gold ring is the speed minimum on the curve; the gold line and ring on the right are the same point on dκ/ds. Zoom enlarges the panel and lets you scroll to the mark.
