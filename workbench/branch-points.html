The numbers

Each probe leaves the pin at 90° to the chain, on whichever side has more room, and runs 40 mm (1:1). “Clearance” is the distance from the probe (after the first 5 mm (1:1), so the pin itself does not count) to the nearest ink in the half, its mirror or the band. Open means at least 3 mm (1:1).

Each is the practical curve re-fitted with that one pin. Pinning costs one or two cubics; the pin is the gold dot, and the branch leaves on the chain's exact heading. A red candidate can still be used with a different branch angle or a shorter reach — the probe is a conservative test, not a prohibition.

How to use it

Choose the point on the exact curve, pin it, then generate the practical curve and attach the branch. The pin is a hard split with the chain's heading (pin error about 2e-7 mm (1:1)), so the junction is exact even though the rest of the curve is a 34-cubic approximation. If a probe is blocked, move along the chain until the dot turns gold; the table is the quick way to see whether the blockage is the mirror, the band, or another winding.
