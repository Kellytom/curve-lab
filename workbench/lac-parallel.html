LAC parallel involutes

One log-aesthetic evolute; every offset is an involute of it. Change α or the offset d.

LAC base evolute E(s) +d involute −d involute

The construction

1. The LAC. Curvature is κ(s) = s^−α, so the tangent angle is θ(s) = s^(1−α)/(1−α) for α ≠ 1 and ln s at α = 1. The curve C(s) is integrated from that heading.

2. The evolute. E(s) = C(s) + ρ(s)N(s), ρ = s^α, N = (−sin θ, cos θ). Every normal of C is a radius of the evolute. The evolute is itself a LAC with α_E = 2 − 1/α: α = −1 (Euler/clothoid) → 3, α = 1 (log spiral) → 1.

3. The involutes. T_E = sign(α)N and the base constant is d₀ = sign(α)ρ(s₀). Every parallel is

Pd(s) = E(s) − (sE(s) + d₀ + d) TE(s)

with d = 0 reconstructing the LAC base and d = ± the two offsets. No normal step of the base is taken after the evolute.

Notes

The pasted getEvolutePoint was a placeholder spiral; this page uses the actual LAC heading and its evolute. The α selector switches between Euler/clothoid, a log spiral and intermediate LACs. α = 0 is excluded: its evolute is a point, so the involute construction has no tangent.

The offset distance is a signed string-length difference; the canvas fits the base, evolute and the two ±d curves. A large d will fold where the offset reaches a centre of curvature; keep d below the smallest radius on the evolute.
